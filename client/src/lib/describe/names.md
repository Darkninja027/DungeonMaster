## Quick Combo Tricks

- **Shop name:** `The [Adjective] [Creature/Object]` → The Drowsy Basilisk, The Gilded Anvil.
- **Location name:** `[Colour/Element] + [Landform]` → Ashmere, Redfen, Frostholm, Goldcrag.
- **Faction name:** `The [Order/Circle/Hand] of the [Adjective] [Noun]` → The Circle of the Pale Lantern.
- **NPC surname:** `[Nature word] + [Body part or tool]` → Oakhand, Stonebrow, Ravenquill, Thistleblade.
