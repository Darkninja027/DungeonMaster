# Purify Food and Drink

*Level 1 transmutation (ritual)*

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Casting Time** | action |
| **Range** | 10 feet |
| **Components** | V, S |
| **Duration** | instantaneous |

You remove poison and rot from nonmagical food and drink in a 5-foot-radius Sphere centered on a point within range.
