# False Life

*Level 1 necromancy*

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Casting Time** | action |
| **Range** | Self |
| **Components** | V, S, M (a drop of alcohol) |
| **Duration** | instantaneous |

You gain 2d4 + 4 Temporary Hit Points.

**At Higher Levels.** You gain 5 additional Temporary Hit Points for each spell slot level above 1.
