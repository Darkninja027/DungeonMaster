### Actions

**Entangling Rope.** Strength Saving Throw: DC 16, one creature the erinyes can see within 120 feet. Failure: 14 (4d6) Force damage, and the target has the Restrained condition until the rope is destroyed, the erinyes uses a Bonus Action to release the target, or the erinyes uses Entangling Rope again.

**Multiattack.** The erinyes makes three Withering Sword attacks and can use Entangling Rope.

**Withering Sword.** Melee Attack Roll: +8, reach 5 ft. 13 (2d8 + 4) Slashing damage plus 11 (2d10) Necrotic damage.

### Reactions

**Parry.** _Trigger:_ The erinyes is hit by a melee attack roll while holding a weapon. _Response:_ The erinyes adds 4 to its AC against that attack, possibly causing it to miss.
